# DECISIONS.md Convention

Every project should have `DECISIONS.md` at root for git-tracked architectural decisions.

**Create/update** when: new project setup, architectural choice, trade-off decision, breaking change.

## Format

```markdown
## [YYYY-MM-DD] Short Title
**Context:** Why needed | **Decision:** What chosen | **Alternatives:** What rejected and why | **Consequences:** Impact going forward
```

One decision per entry. Include rejected alternatives. Keep concise. Read at session start alongside CLAUDE.md.
